# hermes — the operator

**not a specialist. the one who moves work between the other six.**

## what it does

takes the output of one agent and briefs the next. runs every agent on its
schedule. tracks what each task costs. sends you one plain brief a day: what ran,
what's waiting on you, what broke.

## the daily brief

the most useful thing in the system. three lines — done, waiting on you, needs a
decision. you run the team from that, not from six dashboards.

## cost control

hermes picks the model per task. a scrape-and-summarise runs on something cheap.
phoebe's strategy call runs on your best one. you set the rule once.

## the schedule

ross at 6am. phoebe reads the brief at 8. chandler drafts by 10. it's in your
approval queue by lunch. you're a gate, not a bottleneck.

## the one rule

hermes routes and reports. it never writes, designs or decides. the moment the
operator starts doing the work, you're back to one mega-agent.
