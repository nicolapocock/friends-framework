# context — glow lab

## who we are

glow lab makes three products: a gel cleanser, a barrier moisturiser, a spf 50.
started 2024, sold direct and in two pharmacy chains. the pitch is "three things,
done properly" against brands with 40-product ranges.

## who we serve

late-20s to 40s, mostly women, who have tried the 10-step routine and want out.
they know their skin type, they read ingredient lists, and they're tired of being
sold a "glow". they trust a founder who says "you probably don't need this one".

## the offer

the three-product set is 68 gbp, or 22-26 gbp each. free returns. a quiz on site
that will tell you to skip a product if your skin doesn't need it.

## positioning

the skincare brand that talks you out of things.

## proof

- the spf passed a uk spf test at an independent lab (report on the site)
- 4.7 average over 1,900 reviews
- founder is a former formulator at a larger brand — named, with history

## voice

dry, specific, a bit contrarian. short sentences. names ingredients and
percentages. never excited. see voice-guide.md (built from the founder's own
newsletter, 30 issues).
